The Wikipedia Game

Start on one article. Reach another. Links only.

How it works
  1. You are dropped on a Wikipedia article.
  2. You are given a target article.
  3. Get there by clicking links inside the article — nothing else.
  4. No search box, no URL bar, no back-button-into-Google. Fewest clicks and fastest time win.
  5. Back costs five seconds a step and does not undo a click. Explore as much as you like — it goes on the clock, not on your route.
  6. Contents jumps to a section and Find filters the links on the page. Both are free and unlimited — knowing which link to look for is the game, hunting for it in nineteen sections is not.

Everyone who opens the same challenge link races the exact same board — and if their run carried its pace, you race the ghost of it too.
